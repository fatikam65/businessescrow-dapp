✨ BusinessEscrow dApp

A decentralized invoice system on the Sepolia Testnet. Create and pay invoices securely through smart contracts.

🌷 How it works

  • Create invoices with a reference, payee address, and ETH amount
  • Pay invoices — ETH is sent directly to the payee
  • Look up any invoice by its ID on-chain
  • All transactions are immutable on the Ethereum blockchain
